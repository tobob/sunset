# Privacy Policy — Sunset

_Last updated: 2 November 2026_

Sunset is a simple app that shows sunrise and sunset times for your current location. It is built to work entirely on your device.

## Data we collect

**None.** Sunset has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not send any data to the developer or to third parties.

## Location

- Sunset asks for access to your location **only while you are using the app**. It uses the location to calculate sunrise and sunset times.
- The calculation runs **on your device**. Your location is stored only on your device (the last known location, so the app and widget work offline), and you can remove it by deleting the app.
- To show a place name (for example "Warsaw"), the app uses the operating system's built-in geocoding service (Apple on iOS). That request is handled by the operating system under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The developer never receives it.
- You can deny or revoke location access at any time in your device settings. The app then cannot calculate times for a new location.

## Widget

The home and lock screen widget shows the same data and reads it from the app's local storage on your device.

## Children

Sunset does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in this file, with a new date at the top.
